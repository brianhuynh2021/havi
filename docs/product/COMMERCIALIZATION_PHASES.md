# Havi Commercialization Phases

Havi sells operational control, not outcomes. Customers pay to centralize
channels, reduce repetitive work, enforce approvals and avoid missed failures.
Sales material must not promise customers, visits, conversion, growth or
revenue.

## Phase 1 — Customer Zero

Run Havi at Trung Tâm Công Nghệ Nhật Minh using real Facebook content and
Messenger conversations. Record operational failures and workflow friction.

Release criteria:

- external publish and reply states are truthful;
- tenant isolation and role permissions pass integration tests;
- a failed post or expired connection is visible and actionable;
- the founder can complete a normal week without hidden manual steps.

## Phase 2 — Design partners

Onboard a small set of Vietnamese businesses and freelancers with one or two
active social channels. Charge a transparent SaaS fee for the current product,
with no bespoke growth service bundled into the promise.

Learn which daily tasks repeat, where approvals stall, and which provider errors
consume support time.

## Phase 3 — Repeatable self-service

- guided channel connection and reauthorization;
- clear empty, loading, error and recovery states;
- plan limits that correspond to operational usage;
- documented onboarding and support playbooks;
- instrumentation for activation, retention and service reliability.

## Phase 4 — Multi-brand teams

Sell organization-level control to companies and agencies managing several
brands or branches: shared governance, explicit roles, cross-workspace health
and auditable client approvals.

## Phase 5 — Regional platform

Add channels and languages one provider at a time. Each launch requires official
API support, a reliable authorization lifecycle, external confirmation,
reconciliation, localized error guidance and complete automated tests.

Paid promotion remains a provider handoff. Havi may guide and report read-only
status, but it does not spend customer funds or operate campaigns directly.
